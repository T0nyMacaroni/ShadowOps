Questions

AD & cloud review FAQ

Not sure which review fits? We will help you choose.

What access do you need?

For Active Directory, a standard domain user account and a workstation or VPN connection is enough for most of the review. For cloud, a read-only reviewer role in the tenants in scope.

Is this different from an internal pentest?

Yes. An internal pentest proves what an attacker can exploit within a time box. A review is broader and more systematic: it looks at the whole configuration, including risks that are hard to exploit today but dangerous tomorrow.

Will the review affect our users?

No. Data collection is read-only and low-volume. We coordinate timing with your administrators so nothing unexpected shows up in monitoring.

How long does a review take?

Usually five to fifteen days depending on the number of domains, tenants and subscriptions in scope.
