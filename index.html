Approach

How an engagement runs

A clear process with one point of contact, agreed rules of engagement and no surprises along the way.

  1. Scope

    We agree on objectives, targets, rules of engagement and timing in a scoping call.

  2. Recon

    We map your attack surface the way an outside or inside attacker would.

  3. Attack

    We safely exploit and chain weaknesses to show real business impact.

  4. Report

    You receive prioritised findings, evidence and fixes, followed by a debrief.

  5. Retest

    We verify your fixes and confirm the risk is actually closed.

Critical issues are reported to you the moment we confirm them, not weeks later in the final report.
