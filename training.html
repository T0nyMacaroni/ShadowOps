Course tracks

Starting points we shape around you

Every course is customised. These tracks show the ground we cover; we mix modules and set the depth to match your audience.

Web & API hacking

For developers, QA and AppSec teams who want to understand how their applications get broken.

  • OWASP Top 10 and API Top 10 in practice
  • Authentication, authorisation and session flaws
  • Injection, SSRF and deserialisation
  • Secure coding fixes for every attack

Active Directory & infrastructure attacks

For system administrators, engineers and blue teams who manage or defend Windows environments.

  • Enumeration and attack path mapping
  • Kerberos attacks and credential abuse
  • AD CS exploitation
  • Hardening and detection for each technique

Red team tradecraft

For pentesters, red teamers and detection engineers who want to go deeper.

  • Initial access and payload development
  • EDR internals and evasion concepts
  • Command and control infrastructure
  • Operating safely and covertly

Cloud attacks

For cloud engineers and platform teams running Azure, Microsoft 365 or AWS.

  • Entra ID and Azure attack techniques
  • AWS IAM privilege escalation
  • Abusing CI/CD and secrets
  • Cloud logging and detection

Offence for defenders

For SOC analysts and incident responders who want to recognise attacks from the inside.

  • Running the attacks you hunt for
  • Mapping activity to MITRE ATT&CK
  • Writing and testing detection rules
  • Purple team exercises in the lab

Fully custom

  • Based on your own stack where possible
  • From a half-day workshop to a full week

How we tailor it

From first call to final lab

A custom course does not have to take months to prepare. Here is how we get from your goals to a finished training.

  1. Intake

    We discuss your audience, goals, technology and preferred format.

  2. Skills check

    An optional short assessment so we set the right starting level.

  3. Curriculum

    You receive a proposed outline with modules, labs and timing to review.

  4. Delivery

    Hands-on sessions with labs, exercises and an optional capture-the-flag.

  5. Follow-up

    Course materials, lab write-ups and feedback on how your team performed.

Questions

Training FAQ

Tell us what you have in mind and we will tell you what is possible.

How many participants can join?

We recommend six to sixteen participants per trainer so everyone gets individual attention during the labs.

What do participants need?

A laptop with a modern browser and, for some tracks, a virtual machine we provide. Labs are hosted, so no complex setup is needed.

Can the training use our own applications?

Yes. Using your own code or a copy of your environment makes the training even more relevant. We can also build labs that closely resemble it.

Is offensive training safe to give to employees?

Every course covers the legal and ethical framework for security testing, and all exercises take place in isolated lab environments.
