Two complementary disciplines

Test your defences, then make them better

Red teaming measures your resilience without warning. Purple teaming improves it in the open, together with your defenders.

Covert / objective-based

Red teaming

A realistic, goal-driven operation against your organisation as a whole: people, processes and technology. Only a small control group knows the exercise is running.

Typical objectives
Access the payment system, the crown-jewel data or domain control
Frameworks
MITRE ATT&CK, threat-led testing in the spirit of TIBER-EU and DORA TLPT
Duration
Typically 4 to 10 weeks

Threat-informed scenarios

Scenarios built around the actors and techniques most relevant to your sector.

Initial access

Phishing, vishing, exposed services or an assumed-breach starting point when time is tight.

Stealth & evasion

Custom tooling and tradecraft to test your EDR, SIEM and SOC against a careful operator.

Timeline & replay

A full attack timeline mapped to what your defenders saw, so you can replay the gaps.

Collaborative / open-book

Purple teaming

We execute attack techniques step by step while your blue team watches, investigates and builds detections. Improvements land during the engagement, not months after.

Format
Workshops on-site or remote, with your SOC, CSIRT or MSSP
Output
ATT&CK coverage map, tuned rules and repeatable test cases
Duration
From a few days to recurring quarterly cycles

ATT&CK coverage mapping

A clear view of which techniques you prevent, detect, log or miss entirely.

Detection engineering

Together we write and tune detection rules for your SIEM and EDR, such as Sigma or KQL.

Control validation

Proof that the security products you pay for actually trigger when it matters.

Repeatable playbooks

Test cases your team can rerun after every change to stop regressions.

Which one?

Red, purple or a pentest?

Each answers a different question. Many clients start with a pentest, move to purple teaming and test the result with a red team.

// pentest

What is vulnerable?

Broad coverage of a defined scope. Best when you need to find and fix as many weaknesses as possible.

// purple

Can we see it?

Open collaboration to measure and raise detection coverage. Best for maturing a SOC or onboarding new tools.

// red

Would we stop it?

A covert, end-to-end test of people, process and technology. Best when your basics are in place.

// assumed breach

What if they are in?

Skip initial access and start inside. Best value when time or budget is limited.

Questions

Adversary simulation FAQ

We are happy to talk through your situation before you commit to anything.

Is red teaming safe for our business?

Every operation runs under agreed rules of engagement with a control group on your side. We avoid destructive actions, check in at defined moments and can pause at any time.

Are we ready for a red team?

If you have not had regular penetration tests and a functioning detection capability, a red team will mostly confirm what you already suspect. In that case we recommend starting with a pentest or purple teaming.

Do you work with our MSSP or MDR provider?

Yes. Purple teaming is often the best way to verify what your provider detects and how quickly they escalate.

Can you help with DORA TLPT or TIBER-EU?

Our approach follows the same threat-led principles. Talk to us about your regulatory requirements and we will tell you honestly how we can support you.
